The club

Identity & philosophy

From the first kick in 1931 to today’s modern football, Montelune Ballestro stands for intensity, creativity and community.

Playing style

High-tempo football with an emphasis on pressing, quick transitions and expressive attacking combinations in the final third.

Youth academy

A pathway from Montelune’s streets to the first team, built on education, discipline and joy with the ball.

Supporters

The “Lunarios” stand behind the goal, turning every home game into a gold-and-red wall of sound.
